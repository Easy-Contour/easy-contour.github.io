Easy Contour for Mac

Easy Contour is a professional 3D visualization, contouring, and surface modeling package for macOS. Designed for terrain modeling, landscape visualization, surface analysis, contour mapping, 3D mapping, gridding, and advanced geospatial workflows.

Precise Data Editing

Add and edit points, lines, or polygons with intuitive controls. Import, manipulate, and refine datasets seamlessly for accurate surface modeling.

Flexible Rendering Options

Choose from Simple, ClassBreak, or Categories rendering based on attribute tables. Tailor visualizations to reveal patterns and insights effortlessly.

Advanced Symbolization

Point layers support Simple markers or Character markers—enter any character for custom symbols. Elevate your maps with precise, professional styling.

Rich Polygon Fills

Polygon layers offer Simple, Gradient, Hatch, or Picture fills. Create compelling landscapes and surfaces with layered, high-fidelity visuals.

Streamlined Workflow

From data import to 3D export, handle complex projects with native macOS performance. Built for professionals who demand reliability and speed.
